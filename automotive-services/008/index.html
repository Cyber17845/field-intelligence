RULE
Texas finalized right-to-appraisal rules with 20-, 15- and 40-day deadlines for auto claim disputes, covering personal auto policies delivered, issued or renewed on or after March 1, 2027.
Texas Department of Insurance Order No. 2026-10210 adopts 28 TAC §§5.9800-5.9806, the right-to-appraisal rules under Senate Bill 458 for personal auto and residential property policies. Autobody News reported November 12 that TDI lists the order as filed November 4 with an effective date of November 24. The rules apply to policies delivered, issued for delivery or renewed on or after March 1, 2027; TDI had proposed September 1, 2026.
After a party demands appraisal, each side has 20 days to hire an appraiser, an umpire must be chosen within 15 days, and the appraisers must try to agree on the amount of loss within 40 days of the demand. If an umpire is involved, the award is due within 180 days. A demand must be delivered before the policy's suit limitations period expires, and a plaintiff generally cannot demand appraisal after filing suit.
Either side can ask a county or district court where the vehicle is garaged to appoint an umpire, but only the policyholder can invoke the vendor-umpire option, which insurers decide whether to offer. TDI declined to cap umpire fees or require insurers to pay a vendor umpire's full cost, noting that parties usually split umpire costs. Insurers must send a plain-language notice in at least 10-point type when they accept or reject a claim.
Autobody News cited parts sourcing, ADAS calibration allowances, labor rates and total-loss valuations in an October 2025 report as examples of disputes that can trigger appraisal. A separate Consumer Bill of Rights update that mentions appraisal takes effect January 1, 2027.
​
What to do about it: Texas shops, pull your five largest disputed supplements or valuations from the last quarter and mark whether each fight was over labor rate, parts source, calibration or total-loss value. Keep the estimate, photos and invoices in a form you can hand a customer's appraiser inside the 20-day window. For any customer whose policy renews on or after March 1, 2027, have the customer ask the insurer in writing whether it offers a vendor umpire. Give your estimators the four clocks: 20, 15, 40 and 180 days. Shops outside Texas should ask their state insurance department whether it has set appraisal deadlines for auto policies.
Autobody News: Texas finalizes appraisal rules · TDI Commissioner's Order 2026-10210 (adopted rules)

COLLISION REPAIR
Two items
​
Regulation
Rhode Island added ADAS as an eighth required technician certification for licensed body shops; Class A shops must certify every technician holds all eight or show I-CAR Gold Class.
​
Why it matters: Rhode Island's Department of Business Regulation amended its body repair rule effective October 29, Autobody News reported November 23. Class A applicants certify under penalty of perjury that every technician holds all eight certifications; Class B shops need one full set per two technicians with a salvage license, or per five without. A shop out of compliance has 30 days to file a correction plan and 60 more to comply before revocation. The department put two ADAS classes at $62 and $45. Pull each technician's transcript now.
Autobody News: Rhode Island requires ADAS certification
​
Money
Shop satisfaction with automaker certification programs fell for a second straight year to 7.5 out of 10, the lowest since the survey began asking in 2021, Autobody News reported.
​
Why it matters: The "Who Pays for What?" survey of more than 440 shops, about 54% of them certified by at least one automaker, put the 2026 average at 7.5 across roughly two dozen programs, from 8.3 in 2024. BMW-Mini, Mercedes-Benz, Toyota and Lexus rated highest; Hyundai, Kia and Genesis have been among the lowest since 2024. About a dozen shops did not renew any certification in the past 12 months, and many certified shops said they see little return, especially in referrals. Before renewing, list what each certification brought in last year.
Autobody News: OEM certification satisfaction falls

GENERAL AUTO REPAIR
Two items
​
Regulation
BMW filed a second water pump recall covering 720,796 vehicles with the remedy still in development, and Toyota announced a Takata airbag recall of about 314,000 vehicles on November 12.
​
Why it matters: BMW's November 4 report to NHTSA covers 2012-2018 vehicles including the 3 Series, 5 Series, X1 and X3, where water pump connector degradation can let in moisture and cause a short circuit. NHTSA says BMW knows of roughly 10 incidents, all in vehicles repaired under the 2024 recall, and owners are told to park outdoors; the new remedy under campaign 26V733 is still in development. As of November 17 NHTSA's database showed no campaign matching the models in Toyota's Canadian release. Run each VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before quoting a water pump or airbag job.
Autobody News: BMW and Toyota recall roundup
​
Money
Posted mechanical labor rates averaged $161.19 an hour in October, down 0.3% from September and up 5.3% on the year, after declining every month from July through October.
​
Why it matters: Autobody News published the Labor Rate Index, built from National Autobody Research data, on November 23. October body rates averaged $85.58 (up 3.7%), refinish $84.68 (up 3.7%), frame $116.75 (up 4.2%) and mechanical $161.19 (up 5.3%). Of shops that updated surveys in the 12 months to October 31, 51% raised mechanical rates, averaging about 13%, or about $19 an hour. These are rates shops post, not insurer-negotiated rates. Compare your posted mechanical rate with $161.19 and note the date of your last increase.
Autobody News: Labor Rate Index, October

TIRE & WHEEL
Two items
​
Demand
Toyo says North American replacement tire units fell 5% in the first nine months and will end 2026 about 3% below 2025, with a 4% gain expected in the fourth quarter.
​
Why it matters: Modern Tire Dealer posted Toyo's results November 16. North American operating income fell 35.9% to $57.1 million in the nine months, though third-quarter operating income rose nearly 46% to $35.1 million on sales up 8.5%. Toyo cut its full-year North American sales forecast by about $137 million to $2.68 billion and its operating income forecast by about $31 million to $89.9 million, down 21.1% from 2025. Ask your Toyo distributor for fourth-quarter program terms in writing before you set winter inventory.
Modern Tire Dealer: Toyo North American sales rise
​
Demand
GreatWater, a tire dealership with more than 180 locations, announced three acquisitions adding 10 repair locations in Minnesota, Texas and Iowa, after adding 20 shops in the third quarter.
​
Why it matters: Modern Tire Dealer reported November 18 that GreatWater 360 Auto Care bought Motor Works Auto Repair (eight locations, founded 2004 in St. Augusta, Minnesota, taking GreatWater to 10 Minnesota locations), B&S Automotive of Universal City, Texas, and Off Campus Auto Repair of Coralville, Iowa. The B&S and Off Campus deals closed in October and November; prices were not disclosed. If you compete near these sites, price your five best-selling services against theirs. If you are weighing an offer, bring three years of statements current.
Modern Tire Dealer: GreatWater makes three acquisitions

AUTO GLASS
Two items
​
Demand
Caliber Auto Glass said November 16 it acquired Pennsylvania-based Arrow Auto Glass, extending Caliber's footprint to 44 of the lower 48 states; financial terms were not disclosed.
​
Why it matters: Arrow is based in Kingston, Pennsylvania, also does recalibration and adds four states, which Caliber did not name; Arrow's website lists operations in 14 states from Florida to New Hampshire. Autobody News reported November 20 that the two companies will operate separately for now, with Arrow joining Caliber later. Check whether your insurer and fleet agreements name Caliber or Arrow, and ask for current terms in writing.
CollisionWeek: Caliber acquires Arrow Auto Glass · Autobody News: Industry roundup, Nov. 20
​
Regulation
Rhode Island now requires auto glass license applicants to be ADAS calibration certified or use a certified entity, and licensees using another entity must name it to consumers.
​
Why it matters: Autobody News reported November 23 that Rhode Island's Department of Business Regulation amended its auto glass regulation on October 29, the same date it added ADAS as an eighth body technician certification. A licensee that uses another entity for calibration must tell consumers it is not certified and name the entity doing the work. Rhode Island glass shops that sublet calibration should get the partner's certification in writing and add the disclosure to work orders.
Autobody News: Rhode Island requires ADAS certification

TOWING & ROADSIDE
Two items
​
Demand
AAA expects 72.9 million people to drive over Thanksgiving, November 24 to 30, and AAA Mountain West sees battery service calls up about 15% around the holiday.
​
Why it matters: AAA's November 16 forecast puts Thanksgiving travel at 81.5 million, down 0.1% from last year, and car travel at 72.9 million against 73.1 million; nearly 90% will drive. AAA's release names the Tuesday and Wednesday before the holiday and the Sunday and Monday after as the busiest days. ABC reported AAA answered more than half a million roadside calls last Thanksgiving. Over Thanksgiving week, set driver and dispatcher schedules around those four days, and confirm motor club and police-rotation phone lines are staffed through November 30.
AAA Mountain West: Thanksgiving forecast, Nov. 16 · ABC 33/40: AAA expects 81.5M Thanksgiving travelers
​
Supply
Jerr-Dan introduced the JD18, an 18-ton integrated medium-duty wrecker with a 50,000-pound tow capacity, on November 20.
​
Why it matters: Tow Times posted the Jerr-Dan announcement November 20. The JD18, from an Oshkosh business, needs a chassis of at least 33,000 pounds GVWR and has a two-stage boom lifting 36,000 pounds retracted and 15,000 extended, dual 15,000-pound winches with 150 feet of half-inch cable, and a polypropylene body. It targets delivery trucks, small motorhomes, passenger vehicles and SUVs. If you are pricing a medium-duty unit, ask a Jerr-Dan distributor for a written quote including chassis and a delivery date.
Tow Times: Jerr-Dan introduces JD18 wrecker

CAR WASH & DETAILING
Two items
​
Money
A multi-site Michigan Tommy's Express franchisee was sold to family office Desert Lakes Capital in a deal that closed effective November 19; terms were not disclosed.
​
Why it matters: Miracle Car Wash Advisors, the exclusive sell-side advisor, announced the sale November 20 through the International Carwash Association. The seller's eastern Michigan sites were described as having strong unit economics, a loyal membership base and consistent site-level performance. Desert Lakes had recently bought a single Detroit site and now leads Tommy's Express franchisees in greater Detroit. Miracle's advisors say family offices are a growing part of the buyer pool. Owners weighing a sale should pull monthly membership counts, churn and site-level earnings for the last 24 months.
ICA: Miracle advises Tommy's Express franchisee sale

Money
Wash Rescue opened its Ops HQ operations software to all car wash operators on November 16 at $199 per site per month, with a 30-day free trial and no credit card.
​
Why it matters: Wash Rescue's announcement says Ops HQ covers scheduling, time clocking, checklists, maintenance, training and reporting under one login, with integrations to Sonny's, DRB NXT and Washify point-of-sale systems. The $199 price includes all modules and unlimited users; convenience stores, restaurants and storage sites pay $99. For a three-site operator, $199 a site is $597 a month, or $7,164 a year. List what you pay now for scheduling, checklists and maintenance tracking, then compare before the trial ends.

CYBER RISK, PLAINLY
Ransomware, state hackers and AI scams: what small firms face
Criminals go where the money and customer data are. Ransomware is software that locks your files until you pay. The FBI got 3,600-plus ransomware complaints in 2025. Of 1,400-plus from firms outside critical industries, 18% were legal services firms and 17% contractors like electricians.
State-linked: US agencies assess that Iran-affiliated hackers altered equipment controllers at water, energy and local-government sites (updated July 2026). An August advisory lists commercial facilities among sectors being probed. A September fact sheet describes foreign hackers who entered an automation firm in 2025. AI scams: 22,364 FBI complaints mentioned AI in 2025, with losses over $893 million. Cloned voices can ask for wire payments.
In your trade: If a supplier emails new bank details for your parts invoices, confirm the change by phone on a number you already have before you pay.
​
What to do about it: Turn on two-step sign-in wherever you can, starting with email and admins. Keep a backup on a drive not connected to your network, and test it. Confirm any change in bank details by phone, on a number you already had. If a call asks for money, hang up and call back on a number you look up yourself. Install updates. Give people only the access they need. Know who to call; report fraud at ic3.gov. The part this doesn't cover, how your systems are set up, is the day job of our parent company, CyberSainya.
FBI IC3 2025 Internet Crime Report; US government advisories (updated Jul 22, Aug and Sep 23, 2026); FBI IC3 alerts; CISA and FTC guidance.
