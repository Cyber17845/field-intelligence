RULE
Washington voted October 30 to allow propane refrigerants in some HVAC equipment from May 2027, and ACCA, PHCC and HARDI warned November 20 that more states may follow.
Washington's State Building Code Council voted unanimously October 30 to amend its 2024 mechanical code so A3 refrigerants such as R-290 propane can be used in certain HVAC equipment from May 2027. The amendments do not mandate A3. The council limited some installations to experimental applications and required safety-official approval, Contracting Business reported November 6.
ACHR News reported November 20 that ACCA, PHCC and HARDI fear more states will follow the midterms. Democrats gained six state trifectas, for 22 nationally. ACCA is watching new-trifecta states including Michigan, Minnesota, Nevada and Wisconsin, as well as Oregon, Hawaii, Colorado, New Mexico, Illinois, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Maryland, Massachusetts and Maine, for draft rules or campaigns.
New York's amended Part 494 would bar even new A2L refrigerants from 2027 for heat pump water heaters, 2030 for VRF and chillers and 2034 for most other equipment, and HARDI is among the groups litigating. California, Washington and New York already have rules on the books.
ACCA says federal preemption will be hard to achieve in the new Congress, though PHCC also favors it, and HARDI says no bill to amend the AIM Act has been introduced in nearly six years. AHRI's Stephen Yurek wrote November 11 that research on flammable refrigerants in central HVAC is early-stage and firefighter response procedures are lacking.
​
What to do about it: Washington's vote permits A3 equipment but does not require it, so do not buy propane stock or tools for a May 2027 date. Check every state you install in against ACCA's watch list; Maryland is on it. Ask your distributor in writing what R-410A and A2L equipment it will hold through 2027 and on what return terms. If you work in New York, pull Part 494 and match its 2027, 2030 and 2034 dates to the equipment you sell. ACCA says to act before bills are drafted, so sign up for ACCA, PHCC or HARDI state alerts this week. Add a refrigerant-substitution clause to any quote that runs past 2027.

Demand
AHRI's September central air conditioner shipments rose 40.9% to 370,855 units and heat pumps 13.8% to 302,431, while gas furnaces slipped 0.4% to 279,746.
​
Why it matters: ACHR News reported November 13 that combined air conditioner and heat pump shipments were 673,286 in September, up 27.3% from 528,980 a year earlier, and 19% higher in the third quarter. September fell 11.0% from August's 756,739. Third-quarter central air conditioners remain 24.2% below the third quarter of 2024. Gas storage water heaters are down 2.5% for the year. Before the winter rush, ask your distributor for current gas furnace and water heater availability and lead times.
ACHR News: Cooling Shipments Extend Streak to Eight Months · ACHR News: Cooling Shipment Growth Hits 7-Month Streak

Money
ACHR News homeowner research, reported November 17, finds 75% of homeowners are more likely to call a contractor that displays pricing, while only 24% of contractors post prices online.
​
Why it matters: About 45% of homeowners expect a replacement system to cost under $7,500, against $10,000 to $15,000 that contractors report. 58% say only complete equipment failure would prompt them to replace, 54% get three quotes and 32% get two. 71% call online reviews very or extremely important, but only 48% of contractors respond to reviews on third-party sites. This week, put a starting price or price range on your replacement page and answer every open review within two business days.

Supply
Ahead of Thanksgiving, USPS, UPS and FedEx are all closed Thursday, November 26, and last recommended ship dates for Christmas delivery run from December 17 for USPS Ground Advantage to December 23 for overnight services.
​
Why it matters: A November 18 roundup lists UPS 3 Day Select December 21, 2nd Day Air December 22 and Next Day Air December 23; FedEx Express Saver December 21, 2Day December 22 and Overnight December 23; USPS Priority Mail December 18. FedEx's Ground Economy date was December 15, as reported September 24. Carriers add holiday fees through mid-January, and the dates are recommendations, not guarantees. Order parts and equipment for any job due before year-end with your supply house by Wednesday, November 25, and ask for its holiday closing and will-call hours.

Demand
AIA's October Architecture Billings Index scored 44.9, and commercial and industrial billings rose for a second consecutive month, the first growth since 2022, AIA reported November 18.
​
Why it matters: A score below 50 means billings fell. The index was 44.7 in September and 47.2 in August. AIA says institutional and multifamily billings softened significantly this fall, and that billings have not been consistently positive for more than four years. The Northeast was weakest for a fourth month, while the West was essentially flat. This week, sort your open bid log by commercial and industrial against institutional and multifamily, and move estimating hours toward the first group.

ELECTRICAL
Two items
​
Money
Border States' November updates put copper up 35%, 3/4-inch steel up 31% and 4-inch PVC up 21% year over year, with steel suppliers adding 5% to 10% at the start of November.
​
Why it matters: Border States' November 16 supply chain update says copper eased from September's record but stays well above last year, steel and PVC suppliers began November with increases, and data center demand is straining capacity and lead times. Its November 9 commodity update puts PVC lead times at two to four weeks for 2-inch and smaller and eight to 12 weeks for 3-inch and larger, and says suppliers cited higher resin costs. The update calls skilled labor tight. Reprice open bids with copper, conduit or PVC, and order large-diameter PVC now for January starts.
Border States: Supply Chain Update, November 2026 · Border States: Commodity Update, November 2026

Demand
EASA research reported November 19 finds 43% of motor-repair customers saw the number of motors repaired at their facility rise over two years, and 38% rank timely completion first.
​
Why it matters: The survey drew 585 qualified responses from professionals involved in motor repair and replacement decisions, collected February 19 to March 22, 2026. For replacement motors, 44% put application expertise in their top three vendor criteria, 43% delivery time and 31% price. Customers find providers by internet search (48%), referrals (44%), EASA's website (40%) and social media (39%). Quote delivery dates before price on motor jobs, put the completion date in writing, and check that your business listing shows up in an internet search.
EC&M: EASA Research on Motor Repair and Replacement

SOLAR & STORAGE
Two items
​
Demand
SEIA's Energy Storage Market Outlook Q4 2026 puts third-quarter residential battery installs at 746 MWh, up 16% from a year earlier and the largest third quarter on record.
​
Why it matters: Total battery deployments were 14.3 GWh, down 1%, and commercial, industrial and UPS installs rose 29% to 1.3 GWh. Residential is 12% below last year through nine months, because 2025 installs were pulled ahead of the end of the 25D credit, and SEIA now sees 2026 at just under 3 GWh, an 8% decline from 2025. China's Section 301 tariff on non-EV lithium-ion batteries rose from 7.5% to 25% on January 1, 2026. If batteries are your growth line, price Q1 jobs on today's equipment costs.
SEIA: Energy Storage Market Outlook Q4 2026
​
Supply
Commerce's temporary rule, effective September 22, bars importers it finds stockpiling polysilicon products from further entries before December 4, when Section 232 duties under Proclamation 11052 begin.
​
Why it matters: PV Tech reported the rule September 23. It runs September 22 through December 3. New importers registered with CBP on or after August 6 are capped each week at 12 kg of polysilicon, 7 kg of wafers, 2,000 unassembled cells and 55 units of 8541.43.00, cells assembled into modules. Waiver requests go to Polysilicon232@bis.doc.gov, and Commerce aims to answer within 14 days. Ask each module supplier in writing whether its importer is capped or has a waiver pending, and get firm delivery dates for any December jobs.
Federal Register: Restricting Polysilicon Stockpiling · PV Tech: US Introduces Polysilicon Import Limits

COMMERCIAL REFRIGERATION
Two items
​
Money
Producer prices for air conditioning and refrigeration equipment were 4.1% higher in October than a year earlier, while copper and brass mill shapes were up 23.9% and steel mill products 32.9%, BLS reported November 13.
​
Why it matters: BLS puts the October change in refrigeration and air conditioning equipment at 0.1% for the month on a preliminary basis, after 0.4% in August and minus 0.1% in September. Nonferrous wire and cable are up 19.6% over the year. Equipment prices are rising far slower than copper and steel mill prices. Put a 14-day validity date on every replacement and condensing unit quote, and add a clause letting you reprice copper tubing and wire if your supplier's price changes before delivery.
BLS: Producer Price Index Table 2, October 2026 · BLS: Producer Price Index News Release, October 2026
​
Demand
ACHR News said November 21 that the FDA Food Code classifies ice as food, and that cleaning commercial ice machines at least twice a year is a common baseline.
​
Why it matters: The author, who heads a chemical maker, suggests starting at every three months and adjusting at each visit; hard water, high volume and flour or yeast in bakeries, pizzerias and breweries shorten the interval. Neglect shows up as slow harvests, higher energy bills, failed health inspections and compressor wear. Acid cleaner can strip nickel-plated evaporators and void the warranty, and mixed with chlorine sanitizer it can release chlorine gas. Offer your restaurant and bakery accounts a written quarterly clean-and-sanitize plan with EPA-registered sanitizers.
ACHR News: Cleaning Commercial Ice Machines

CYBER RISK, PLAINLY
Ransomware, state hackers and AI scams: what small firms face
Criminals go where the money and customer data are. Ransomware is software that locks your files until you pay. The FBI got 3,600-plus ransomware complaints in 2025. Of 1,400-plus from firms outside critical industries, 18% were legal services firms and 17% contractors like electricians.
State-linked: US agencies assess that Iran-affiliated hackers altered equipment controllers at water, energy and local-government sites (updated July 2026). An August advisory lists commercial facilities among sectors being probed. A September fact sheet describes foreign hackers who entered an automation firm in 2025. AI scams: 22,364 FBI complaints mentioned AI in 2025, with losses over $893 million. Cloned voices can ask for wire payments.
In your trade: A federal advisory on Siemens controllers lists commercial facilities among sectors being probed; keep equipment controllers off the open internet.
​
What to do about it: Turn on two-step sign-in wherever you can, starting with email and admins. Keep a backup on a drive not connected to your network, and test it. Confirm any change in bank details by phone, on a number you already had. If a call asks for money, hang up and call back on a number you look up yourself. Install updates. Give people only the access they need. Know who to call; report fraud at ic3.gov. The part this doesn't cover, how your systems are set up, is the day job of our parent company, CyberSainya.
FBI IC3 2025 Internet Crime Report; US government advisories (updated Jul 22, Aug and Sep 23, 2026); FBI IC3 alerts; CISA and FTC guidance.
